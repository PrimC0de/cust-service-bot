Deposit Methods, Limits, and Fees

Deposit method limits and requirements:

Bank transfer (網銀):
- Minimum: 1,000 per transaction
- Some channels require 5,000 minimum for first-time or specific bank routes — this is set by the payment processor, not arbitrary
- Maximum: 200,000 per transaction
- Daily limit: 500,000
- Fee: free (platform absorbs processing cost)

ATM payment:
- Minimum: 500
- Maximum: 30,000 per transaction
- Fee: free

Convenience-store (超商):
- Minimum: 1,000
- Maximum: 20,000 per transaction
- Fee: 25–35 depending on store chain

USDT:
- Minimum: 10 USDT
- Maximum: no platform cap
- Network fee paid by sender

If online banking requires a 5,000 minimum, it means that specific payment route has a processor minimum. Try ATM payment or convenience-store deposit for smaller amounts, or deposit 5,000 or more via bank transfer.

Limits may vary by VIP level. Check Wallet > Deposit for your current applicable limits.
