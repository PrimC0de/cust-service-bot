Funds Not Credited

If your deposit has not been credited (上分):

Expected processing times:
- Bank transfer: 5–30 minutes
- ATM / convenience-store: 10–60 minutes
- USDT: 10–30 minutes after network confirmations

If your deposit is still not credited after the expected time:

1. Check Wallet > Deposit History for the order status (Pending / Processing / Failed).
2. Verify you paid the exact amount shown on the deposit form.
3. Confirm you used the correct account number, payment code, or USDT address.
4. For bank transfers, ensure the transfer was made AFTER generating the deposit order.

If status shows Pending beyond 60 minutes:
- Submit proof via Wallet > Deposit History > Submit Proof
- Or contact customer service with: member ID, deposit amount, payment time, and receipt screenshot

Common reasons for delay:
- Amount mismatch between form and actual transfer
- Transfer made without generating a deposit order first
- Payment outside business hours for manual-review methods
- Bank processing delay during peak hours

Funds are not lost — unmatched deposits are held for manual matching once proof is provided.
