Incorrect Amount or Payment Details

If the amount you transferred does not match the amount entered on the deposit form (e.g. transferred 25,000 but selected 20,000 on the form):

1. Do NOT submit a new deposit order for the difference yet.
2. Go to Wallet > Deposit History and locate the original order.
3. Tap "Report Amount Mismatch" or contact customer service with:
   - Original deposit order number
   - Amount shown on the form
   - Actual amount transferred
   - Bank transfer receipt or payment proof

The excess amount (e.g. extra 5,000) will be manually matched and credited to your account after verification. This typically takes 10–30 minutes during business hours.

If you transferred LESS than the form amount, the deposit may fail auto-matching. Submit proof and the credited amount will reflect what was actually received.

Always double-check the amount on the deposit form before completing payment. Generate a new deposit order if you need to change the amount — do not transfer a different amount against an existing order.

For repeated mismatches, customer service may advise using convenience-store or ATM payment where the exact amount is entered at the payment terminal.
