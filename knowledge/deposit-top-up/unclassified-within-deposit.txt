General Deposit Questions

For deposit questions that do not fit a specific issue:

Deposit timing:
- Normal processing: 5–60 minutes depending on method
- Peak hours (evenings, weekends): may take up to 90 minutes
- During maintenance: deposits pause and resume automatically after maintenance ends
- "Will deposit take long right now?" — check Service Status page for current queue times

Bank transfer and convenience store:
- Bank transfer and convenience-store deposit are separate methods
- Bank transfer does NOT need to be linked to a convenience store
- Each method has its own flow under Wallet > Deposit

General tips:
- Always generate a deposit order BEFORE making payment
- Pay the exact amount shown
- Use the payment code or account number from the current order only
- Do not reuse old deposit addresses or account numbers

If unsure which deposit method to use, bank transfer is recommended for amounts over 5,000; convenience-store or ATM for smaller amounts or when bank limits are reached.

Contact customer service with your member ID for any deposit question not resolved by the above.
