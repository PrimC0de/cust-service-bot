Payment Card Application and Cancellation

The platform payment card is optional. You are not required to apply for or use the card to deposit, play, or withdraw.

If you have not submitted a card application, no card will be issued. If you see a card-related notification but did not apply, it may be a system prompt — you can dismiss it without action.

Application process:
1. Go to Account > Payment Card.
2. Review terms and submit application with required identity verification.
3. Approval typically takes 1–3 business days.

If your application is not approved, it is automatically cancelled — no card is issued and no fees apply. You may re-apply after 30 days if your account meets updated eligibility criteria.

To cancel a pending application before approval, go to Account > Payment Card > Cancel Application.

If you do not intend to use the card, simply do not complete the application. An unapproved or cancelled application has no impact on your account balance or other services.
