Promotion Eligibility and Rejected Applications

If your promotion or bonus application shows as failed, common reasons include:
- Accidental tap before meeting eligibility requirements (e.g. first deposit not yet completed)
- Already claimed the same promotion within the eligibility period
- Account not fully verified
- Promotion quota exhausted for the day

For first-deposit promotions: you must complete your first successful deposit before applying. If you tapped apply before depositing, the application will fail. After your first deposit clears, you may re-apply if the promotion is still active.

To resolve a failed application:
1. Check the promotion rules on the Promotions page.
2. Confirm your deposit has been credited.
3. Wait 10 minutes and try applying again if eligible.
4. Contact customer service with your member ID and the promotion name if the issue persists.

Rejected applications do not affect your account status. You remain eligible for other active promotions that match your account profile.
