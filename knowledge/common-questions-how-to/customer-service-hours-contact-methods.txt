Customer Service Hours and Contact Methods

Customer service is available to assist with account, deposit, withdrawal, and platform questions.

Service hours: 09:00 – 02:00 daily (next day), including weekends and holidays.

Contact methods:
1. Live chat: tap the chat icon on the website or app (fastest response)
2. Official LINE account: search for our verified LINE ID shown on the Contact page
3. Telegram: use the official support bot linked from the Help Center
4. Email: support listed on the Contact page (response within 24 hours)

When asking "which account is customer service?" — use only contacts listed on the official website Help Center > Contact Us. Do not trust accounts shared in unsolicited messages.

For faster resolution, have ready:
- Your member ID
- Registered phone number
- Description of the issue
- Screenshots or receipt if applicable

Average live chat response time: under 3 minutes during service hours. Messages sent outside hours are answered when service reopens.

Emergency issues (account locked with balance, large uncredited deposit): mark your message as urgent in live chat.
