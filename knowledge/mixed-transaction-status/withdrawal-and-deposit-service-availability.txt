Deposit and Withdrawal Service Availability

To check whether deposit and withdrawal services are operating normally:

1. Visit the platform homepage or login page — any service disruption is announced via banner notification.
2. Check the Service Status section under Help Center for real-time updates on:
   - Bank transfer deposits
   - Convenience-store (超商) deposits
   - USDT deposits
   - ATM payment deposits
   - Withdrawals to bank accounts

Normal processing times when services are available:
- Bank transfer deposit: 5–30 minutes
- Convenience-store deposit: 10–60 minutes
- USDT deposit: 10–30 minutes after network confirmations
- Withdrawal: 30 minutes to 4 hours depending on bank

If both deposit and withdrawal seem slow, there may be scheduled maintenance or high transaction volume. Maintenance is typically announced in advance and lasts 30 minutes to 2 hours.

For combined status questions ("are withdrawals and store deposits both working?"), check the Service Status page or ask customer service for the latest update.
