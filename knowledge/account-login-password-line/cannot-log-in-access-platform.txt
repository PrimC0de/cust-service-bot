Cannot Log In or Access Platform

If you cannot open or log in to the platform, try these steps:

1. Clear browser cache or restart the app.
2. Check your internet connection and try a different network.
3. Ensure you are using the correct registered phone number or username.
4. If you see "IP blocked" or "login restricted," your IP may be flagged. Switch to mobile data or contact customer service to review the restriction.
5. Wait 15 minutes if you exceeded login attempt limits.

If the platform cannot be opened directly after a deposit, your account may be under temporary review. Deposited funds are not lost — they remain in your account once access is restored. Contact customer service with your member ID and deposit receipt for priority assistance.

Maintenance windows are announced on the login page. During maintenance, access is temporarily unavailable.

For persistent login failures, provide your registered phone number and approximate last successful login time to customer service for account verification.
