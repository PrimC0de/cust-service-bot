Account Closure and Other Account Requests

Account closure (注销) is available upon request for members with zero pending balance and no active promotions.

To request account closure:
1. Withdraw all remaining balance.
2. Cancel or complete any active promotions.
3. Contact customer service with your member ID and closure request.
4. Confirm identity via SMS verification.
5. Account is closed within 3 business days after confirmation.

Once closed, the account cannot be reopened. A new registration would require new identity verification.

Other account requests not covered by specific categories (e.g. general account inquiries, data export requests) should be directed to customer service with a clear description of your need.

LINE binding issues: go to Account Settings > LINE Binding to link or unlink. If LINE login fails, try password login and rebind LINE from settings.

For any account question without a clear category, customer service will route your request to the appropriate team within 24 hours.
