Change Phone, Name, Profile, and Account Binding

To update account-related information:

Change bank account (passbook account):
1. Go to Account Settings > Bank Account.
2. Submit a change request with photos of the new passbook and your ID.
3. Review takes 1–2 business days. Deposits to the old account during review may require manual matching.

Rebind account:
You may rebind your phone number or bank account after identity verification. Go to Account Settings > Security > Rebind and follow the verification steps. Only one active binding per type is allowed.

Change phone number:
Requires SMS verification on the old number. If the old number is unavailable, contact customer service with ID verification documents.

Change name or profile info:
Submit an update request under Account Settings > Personal Info with matching ID documentation. Name must match your verified identity document.

Processing time for profile changes is typically 1–3 business days. Withdrawals may be paused during bank account change review.
