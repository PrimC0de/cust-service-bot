Forgot Password / Password Recovery

Passwords cannot be viewed directly for security reasons. To reset your password:

1. On the login page, tap "Forgot Password."
2. Enter your registered phone number.
3. Receive an SMS verification code (valid for 5 minutes).
4. Enter the code and set a new password (minimum 8 characters, include letters and numbers).
5. Log in with your new password.

If you do not receive the SMS code:
- Confirm the phone number matches your registered number
- Check SMS is not blocked by your carrier
- Wait 60 seconds and tap "Resend Code"
- Contact customer service if the registered number is no longer in use

For app users: go to Login > Forgot Password and follow the same SMS verification flow.

Never share your password or verification code with anyone, including customer service staff. Support will never ask for your password.
