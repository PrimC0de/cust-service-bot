Account Locked, Suspended, or Disabled

If your account has been closed, suspended, or placed on a blacklist, this typically results from:
- Terms of service violations (multiple accounts, bonus abuse, suspicious activity)
- Incomplete or failed identity verification
- Chargeback or payment disputes
- Extended inactivity with unresolved compliance issues

To appeal or request review:
1. Contact customer service with your member ID and registered phone number.
2. Explain your situation and provide any supporting documents requested.
3. Review typically takes 3–7 business days.

During suspension, you cannot log in, deposit, or place bets. If funds remain in the account, they are held pending review outcome. Approved appeals may restore access; denied appeals may result in account closure per platform policy.

Blacklist status is applied after investigation. Customer service can explain the specific reason tied to your account and whether reinstatement is possible.

For urgent balance inquiries on a suspended account, email support with your ID verification documents.
