Birthday Reward Policy

Members who have completed identity verification and made at least one successful deposit within the past 90 days are eligible for the birthday reward.

How to apply:
1. Log in to your account during your birthday month.
2. Go to Promotions > Birthday Reward.
3. Tap "Apply Now" and confirm your registered birth date matches your ID.
4. The reward is credited within 24 hours after approval.

Birthday promotions may include bonus credit, free spins, or red packet rewards depending on your VIP level. Standard members receive a fixed birthday bonus; VIP members receive an enhanced package.

If you do not see the birthday reward option, ensure your profile birth date is correct under Account Settings > Personal Info. The reward can only be claimed once per calendar year.

For questions about available birthday activities or bonus amounts, check the Promotions page or contact customer service during your birthday month.
