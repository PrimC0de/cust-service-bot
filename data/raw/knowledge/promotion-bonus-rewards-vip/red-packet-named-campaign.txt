Named Campaigns and Red Packet Promotions

The platform runs seasonal campaigns including returning-member offers, red packet events, and named deposit bonuses.

Returning-member promotion example: deposit 1,000 and receive 500 bonus credit. Eligibility requires:
- Account registered before the campaign start date
- No login activity for at least 30 days prior to the campaign
- Minimum single deposit of 1,000 during the campaign period
- One claim per member per campaign

Red packet campaigns are announced on the Promotions page and via in-app notifications. To participate, tap the campaign banner and follow the deposit or wagering requirements listed.

Campaign terms vary by event. Always read the full rules on the promotion detail page before participating. Bonus credit from campaigns is subject to turnover requirements before withdrawal.

If a campaign shows as expired, it cannot be reactivated. Check current active promotions under Promotions > Active Events.
