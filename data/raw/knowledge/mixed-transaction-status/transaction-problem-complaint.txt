Transaction Problem or Complaint

## Intent

The customer reports a failed, delayed, incorrect, or confusing transaction or service issue. Typical wording includes: kok belum masuk, salah, gagal, lama, ga bisa, uangnya mana, and tolong cek.

## Required Flow

1. Use the current conversation to identify the specific transaction and problem.
2. Acknowledge that specific problem without adding unverified facts.
3. Give the concrete next step supported by the available transaction status or knowledge.
4. Ask for proof and an order ID only when those details are genuinely needed.

Escalation is appropriate for money discrepancies, repeated failures, disputes, fraud signals, angry customers, or unknown transaction truth when a human-handoff channel exists.

Never say that the transaction is being checked or escalated unless the corresponding tool or handoff actually ran. Do not confirm success until completion is verified.
