Duplicate and Multiple Accounts Policy

Each person is permitted one account only. The platform does not allow multiple accounts for the same individual.

If you and a household member each have an account (e.g. one account for you, one for your spouse), both accounts must:
- Use separate verified identity documents
- Use separate phone numbers and bank accounts
- Not share devices or IP addresses during registration

Having two accounts under the same identity violates platform terms and may result in account suspension or bonus forfeiture.

If you accidentally created a duplicate account, contact customer service to identify the primary account. The duplicate may be closed and any eligible balance transferred to the primary account after verification.

Do not attempt to log in to both accounts from the same device simultaneously. If you need help consolidating accounts, provide both member IDs and explain the situation to customer service.
