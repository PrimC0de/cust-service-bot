Daily Task Not Completed After Wagering

Daily tasks require specific actions to be recognized by the system. If you completed sports bets but the task still shows incomplete:

Common causes:
1. Bets placed outside the daily task entry point — you must enter sports betting THROUGH the daily task banner/link for the task to track wagers.
2. Minimum bet amount not met — each task specifies a minimum wager per bet (e.g. 500 per bet).
3. Bet type not eligible — some tasks require specific bet types (e.g. pre-match only, minimum odds 1.5).
4. Settlement delay — bets must be settled (won or lost) before counting toward task progress.
5. Task period expired — daily tasks reset at 00:00 platform time.

Correct procedure for sports wagering tasks:
1. Go to Promotions > Daily Tasks.
2. Tap the specific sports betting task (do NOT navigate to sports separately).
3. Place the required number of bets at the required amount through the task-linked page.
4. Wait for bet settlement — progress updates within 5–15 minutes after settlement.

If you placed two successful 500 bets via the task entry but progress is stuck, contact customer service with:
- Task name and date
- Bet slip IDs or screenshots
- Time bets were placed

Support can manually verify and credit task completion if system tracking failed.
