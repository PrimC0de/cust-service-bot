Rules, Terms, and Policy

Platform rules and turnover (流水) requirements:

Turnover / wagering requirement:
- Deposited funds typically require 1x turnover before withdrawal ( wager total equal to deposit amount)
- Promotional bonus credit requires higher turnover (1x–15x depending on promotion)
- Turnover = total valid bet amount, regardless of win or loss
- Example: deposit 10,000 with 1x turnover requirement → you must place 10,000 in total valid bets before withdrawing

To check your current turnover progress:
1. Go to Wallet > Turnover Status
2. View "Required" vs "Completed" amounts
3. Remaining turnover must reach zero before withdrawal of associated funds

Insurance payout (保險赔付):
- A feature in certain games that returns a portion of losses under specific conditions
- Not actual insurance — it is a platform rebate mechanism
- Terms vary by game; check the game rules page for eligibility and payout percentage
- Insurance payout credit may have its own turnover requirement

General terms:
- One account per person
- Minimum age 18
- All bets are final once confirmed
- Platform reserves the right to void bets in case of obvious errors or system malfunctions
