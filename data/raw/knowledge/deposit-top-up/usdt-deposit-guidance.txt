USDT Deposit Guidance

To deposit using USDT:

1. Log in and go to Wallet > Deposit > USDT.
2. Select the network (TRC20 recommended for lower fees; ERC20 also supported).
3. Copy the deposit address shown — do NOT use an address from a previous deposit.
4. Send USDT from your external wallet to the copied address.
5. Wait for network confirmations (TRC20: ~1–5 minutes; ERC20: ~5–15 minutes).
6. Funds are credited automatically after confirmations.

Important warnings:
- Send only USDT on the selected network. Sending other tokens or wrong network may result in permanent loss.
- Minimum deposit: 10 USDT. Amounts below minimum may not be credited.
- The deposit address may change periodically. Always copy a fresh address from the deposit page.
- Do not send from an exchange that does not support memo/tag if required.

If your USDT deposit is not credited after 30 minutes and sufficient confirmations, contact customer service with:
- Transaction hash (TXID)
- Network used
- Amount sent
- Screenshot of the transfer

USDT deposits are available 24/7 unless announced otherwise on the Service Status page.
