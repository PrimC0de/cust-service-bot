How to Deposit

Available deposit methods:
1. Bank transfer (網銀轉帳)
2. ATM payment / ATM繳費
3. Convenience-store payment (超商儲值)
4. USDT (crypto)

General steps for bank transfer:
1. Log in and go to Wallet > Deposit.
2. Select your preferred method.
3. Enter the deposit amount and confirm.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete payment.
5. Funds are credited after the system matches your payment.

ATM payment (when bank transfer limit is reached):
1. Go to Wallet > Deposit > ATM Payment.
2. Select the bill payment option and note the payment code.
3. Visit an ATM or convenience store that supports bill payment.
4. Enter the code and pay the exact amount shown.
5. Keep your receipt until the deposit is credited.

If you have reached your daily bank transfer limit, ATM payment and convenience-store deposit are alternative options that do not use your bank transfer quota.

Contact customer service only if payment was completed but not credited after the expected processing time.
