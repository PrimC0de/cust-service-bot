Proof Submitted and Manual Posting

Most deposits are credited automatically once the system matches your payment. You do NOT need to contact customer service for every deposit.

Manual posting is required only in these cases:
- Convenience-store deposit where the receipt could not be auto-matched
- Bank transfer with incorrect reference number
- Deposit amount does not match the submitted form amount
- Payment made outside the deposit flow (direct transfer without generating a deposit order)

When manual posting is needed:
1. Go to Wallet > Deposit History > Submit Proof.
2. Upload a clear photo of your payment receipt or bank transfer screenshot.
3. Include the deposit order number if available.
4. Processing takes 10–30 minutes during business hours.

For standard bank transfers and USDT deposits completed through the normal deposit flow, crediting is automatic. Do not message customer service unless the expected processing time has passed.

Business hours for manual review: 09:00–02:00 daily. Submissions outside hours are processed at the start of the next business period.
