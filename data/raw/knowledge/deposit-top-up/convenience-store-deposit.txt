Convenience-Store Deposit Issues

Convenience-store (超商) deposit procedure:
1. Go to Wallet > Deposit > Convenience Store.
2. Select store chain (7-Eleven, FamilyMart, etc.) and enter amount.
3. Receive a payment code and barcode.
4. Visit the store, scan barcode, and pay at the counter.
5. Keep the printed receipt until credited.

Lost receipt / forgot which store:
If you lost the payment receipt but have ID documentation:
1. Contact customer service with your member ID, approximate payment date/time, and payment amount.
2. Provide a photo of your ID for verification.
3. Support can trace the payment through the store chain's settlement system using your member ID and payment timestamp.
4. Processing takes 1–4 hours depending on store chain confirmation.

We cannot look up the store location without a receipt or approximate time, but settlement records can match payments by member ID and amount within 24 hours of payment.

If payment was made but not credited and you have no receipt, submit whatever proof you have (bank notification, store SMS) via Wallet > Deposit History > Submit Proof.

Store payment tracing is available during business hours 09:00–02:00.
