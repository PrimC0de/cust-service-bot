Top-up and Deposit Flow

## Intent

The customer wants to add balance or credit and may need to know the amount or active payment destination.

Typical wording includes: topup, depo, isi saldo, mau masuk, rek mana, and transfer kemana.

## Required Flow

1. Ask for the amount only when the customer has not provided it.
2. Return a payment destination only when it comes from approved system or configuration data supplied to the bot.
3. Ask the customer to send proof after transferring.
4. Confirm that funds were credited only after payment verification and fulfillment are confirmed.

Never invent a payment destination, account number, transaction status, or completion state. If the required system data is unavailable, say that it cannot be confirmed right now.

## Response Style

Keep the reply brief and match the customer's tone. For example, when the customer clearly addresses the bot as "bos", it may ask: "Siap bos, mau top berapa?"
