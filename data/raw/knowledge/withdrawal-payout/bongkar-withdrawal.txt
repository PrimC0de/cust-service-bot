Bongkar or Withdrawal Request

## Intent

"Bongkar" may mean withdrawing, converting, or cashing out a balance. Similar wording includes tarik, WD, cairin, mau keluar, and proses bongkar.

## Required Flow

1. Identify the requested withdrawal amount.
2. Collect only missing details such as member ID and the destination bank account or e-wallet.
3. Submit or report a withdrawal status only when the relevant account, balance, eligibility, request, and fulfillment data are actually available.
4. Confirm payment only after payout completion is confirmed.

Do not claim to check, submit, process, or complete a withdrawal unless that action actually occurred. If the bot has no transaction tools or verified status, clearly say that it cannot confirm the withdrawal right now.

Escalation is required for an account mismatch, insufficient balance, policy exception, or processing failure.
