Withdrawal Pending or Not Received

Normal withdrawal processing times:
- Standard: 30 minutes to 2 hours
- Peak periods: up to 4 hours
- First withdrawal or large amount: up to 24 hours for security review

If your withdrawal has been pending for a long time:

1. Check Wallet > Withdrawal History for status (Pending / Processing / Completed / Rejected).
2. Verify your bank account details are correct under Account Settings.
3. Confirm you have completed any required turnover for bonus-related withdrawals.
4. Check if your bank is under maintenance (some banks pause incoming transfers overnight).

Status meanings:
- Pending: in queue, will process shortly
- Processing: sent to bank, awaiting bank confirmation
- Completed: funds sent — check your bank account (may take additional 15–30 min to appear)
- Rejected: see rejection reason; funds returned to your platform balance

If pending beyond 4 hours:
Contact customer service with withdrawal order number, amount, and registered bank account last 4 digits.

Common delays:
- Bank maintenance window (23:00–07:00 for some banks)
- Name mismatch between platform account and bank account
- Daily withdrawal limit reached (resets at 00:00)
- Security review for first large withdrawal

Your funds are safe during pending status — they are not lost.
