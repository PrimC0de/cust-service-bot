Bonus Withdrawal Limits

Promotional and bonus credit has separate withdrawal rules from your main balance:

General bonus withdrawal rules:
- Bonus credit must meet turnover (wagering) requirements before withdrawal
- Standard turnover: 1x–15x depending on promotion type
- Maximum withdrawable amount from bonus may be capped (e.g. 5x the bonus amount)
- Bonus and main balance withdraw separately

To check your bonus withdrawal limit:
1. Go to Wallet > Bonus Balance.
2. View "Withdrawable Amount" and "Remaining Turnover Required."
3. The withdrawable amount updates automatically as you complete wagering.

If you ask "is there a limit on promotional credit withdrawal?" — yes:
- You can only withdraw bonus-derived winnings up to the promotion's maximum payout cap
- Uncompleted turnover blocks withdrawal of bonus-related funds
- Some promotions restrict withdrawal to main balance only after turnover is complete

VIP members may have higher bonus withdrawal caps. Check your promotion terms under Promotions > My Bonuses for specific limits on active offers.

Contact customer service with your promotion name for exact turnover and withdrawal cap details.
