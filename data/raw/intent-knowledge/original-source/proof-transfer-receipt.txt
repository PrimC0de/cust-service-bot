Proof of Transfer / Receipt Intent Playbook

Intent ID: INT_PROOF_TRANSFER
Priority: P0
Source type: Observed frontliner-derived taxonomy

## Retrieval Profile

Customer goal: Submit proof and get acknowledgement/verification.
Classification cues: image; nih bukti; udah transfer; cek ya; ini struknya
Semantic retrieval text: Intent: Proof of Transfer / Receipt. Customer goal: Submit proof and get acknowledgement/verification. Typical phrases: image; nih bukti; udah transfer; cek ya; ini struknya. Meaning: customer says payment was sent or provides a receipt/proof for verification.

## Required Context and Routing

Required context or entities: receipt/image; transaction reference; amount; destination; duplicate/freshness indicators
Default bot action: Acknowledge naturally, verify evidence and mismatches, confirm completion only after fulfillment.
Backend or tool requirement: Image ingestion; receipt extraction; duplicate/freshness/reference/amount/destination checks
Escalation triggers: Unreadable, duplicate, mismatch, suspected edit/forgery, or no matching transaction.
Success signal: Proof matched and verified

## Behavior Rules

### Receipt received
Required action: Acknowledge and verify.
Do not: Do not confirm completion before backend/system confirmation.
Required data or tool: Image/receipt verification + matching transaction
Example response: “Oke bos, bukti udah saya terima. Bentar saya cek dulu.”

### Receipt unreadable
Required action: Ask for a clearer/full image.
Do not: Do not infer unreadable values.
Required data or tool: Image ingestion
Escalate if: Repeated unreadable evidence
Example response: “Fotonya kurang kebaca bos, boleh kirim ulang yang full?”

### Receipt mismatch
Required action: Stop repeat payment and investigate mismatch.
Do not: Do not tell customer to transfer again.
Required data or tool: Amount/destination/reference checks
Escalate if: Mismatch, duplicate, suspected edit/forgery
Example response: “Nominal di bukti beda sama order bos. Saya cek dulu ya, jangan transfer lagi dulu.”

## Ambiguity Guidance

### Compared with Transaction Status (INT_TX_STATUS)
Why they overlap: “udah transfer” may be either a proof submission or a status check.
Prefer this intent when: User attaches/sends receipt or asks to verify evidence.
Prefer Transaction Status when: User asks whether it has arrived/completed without providing evidence.
Context to check: Attachment presence; previous bot request for proof; explicit “cek bukti” wording
Classifier guidance: Attachment/request-for-verification strongly favors Proof of Transfer.

### Compared with Top Up / Deposit (INT_TOPUP)
Why they overlap: “udah transfer” can occur during the top-up workflow.
Prefer this intent when: User explicitly provides proof/receipt or asks for proof verification.
Prefer Top Up / Deposit when: User is still initiating/following the top-up flow and no proof is presented.
Context to check: Prior assistant message; attachment presence
Classifier guidance: Use state/history; do not classify each message independently when a flow is active.

## Response Style Examples

Receipt received: “Oke bos, bukti udah saya terima. Bentar saya cek dulu.”
Unreadable: “Fotonya kurang kebaca bos, boleh kirim ulang yang full?”
Mismatch: “Nominal di bukti beda sama order bos. Saya cek dulu ya, jangan transfer lagi dulu.”
Only after system confirmation: “Udah masuk bos ✅”
