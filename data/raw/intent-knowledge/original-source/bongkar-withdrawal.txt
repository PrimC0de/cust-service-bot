Bongkar / Withdrawal Intent Playbook

Intent ID: INT_WITHDRAWAL
Priority: P0
Source type: Observed frontliner-derived taxonomy

## Retrieval Profile

Customer goal: Withdraw/convert balance and understand requirements/status.
Classification cues: bongkar; tarik; wd; cairin; mau keluar; proses bongkar; minimal bongkar; id bongkar; harga bongkaran; rate bongkar; tanpa order
Semantic retrieval text: Intent: Bongkar / Withdrawal. Customer goal: Withdraw/convert balance and understand requirements/status. Typical phrases: bongkar; tarik; wd; cairin; mau keluar; proses bongkar. Meaning: customer wants to withdraw or cash out balance.

## Required Context and Routing

Required context or entities: amount; customer/account ID; destination account/e-wallet; balance/eligibility; transaction context
Default bot action: Identify request, collect only missing required details, submit safely, and report actual status.
Backend or tool requirement: Balance/account lookup; withdrawal request; eligibility checks; fulfillment status
Escalation triggers: Account mismatch, insufficient balance, policy exception, or processing failure.
Success signal: Withdrawal accepted and actual completion confirmed

## Behavior Rules

### Approved bongkar details
Minimum bongkar: 1B.
Send to ID: 405060.
ID bongkar: 405060, Coin House2.

Required customer details:

- ID and account name
- Amount
- Bank account or e-wallet number
- Payment method
- Account holder name

Ask the customer to check the destination account number again. Payment is made by copying the account number exactly as supplied.

### Bongkar rates
- Harga bongkaran: X60/B
- Tanpa order di kami: X55/B

Keep the rate notation exactly as supplied. Do not infer a different calculation when the requested amount or applicable rate is unclear.

### Requests that are not processed
Do not process a request with the wrong sender ID, wrong amount, retail/eceran amount, wrong destination, or a submission more than six hours old.

### Amount missing
Required action: Ask withdrawal amount.
Do not: Do not infer amount.
Required data or tool: Conversation context
Example response: “Siap bos, bongkar berapa?”

### Required details missing
Required action: Collect only missing account/destination details.
Do not: Do not request unnecessary sensitive data.
Required data or tool: Account/balance/eligibility lookup
Escalate if: Account mismatch or insufficient balance
Example response: “ID + nominal + rekening/e-wallet tujuan kirim ya.”

### Request submitted / processing
Required action: Report actual status from backend.
Do not: Do not claim payout complete while pending.
Required data or tool: Withdrawal request + fulfillment status
Escalate if: Policy exception or processing failure
Example response: “Masih proses bos, belum selesai ya.”

## Ambiguity Guidance

No specific ambiguity pair is defined.

## Response Style Examples

First: “Siap bos, bongkar berapa?”
If details missing: “ID + nominal + rekening/e-wallet tujuan kirim ya.”
After customer sends details/proof: “Oke bos, saya cek dulu ya.”
If still pending: “Masih proses bos, belum selesai ya.”
Only after payout confirmed: “Udah dibayar bos ✅”

Dialogue examples guide tone and behavior only. They are not proof of live status or factual truth. Live facts must come from approved configuration or backend tools.
