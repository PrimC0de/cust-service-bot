Payment / Account Details Intent Playbook

Intent ID: INT_PAYMENT_DETAILS
Priority: P0
Source type: Observed frontliner-derived taxonomy

## Retrieval Profile

Customer goal: Get correct bank/account/payment destination.
Classification cues: rek mana; nomor rekening; bank apa; kirim ke mana; rekening yang mana
Semantic retrieval text: Intent: Payment / Account Details. Customer goal: Get correct bank/account/payment destination. Typical phrases: rek mana; nomor rekening; bank apa; kirim ke mana; rekening yang mana. Meaning: customer asks where or to which account/payment destination to send funds.

## Customer Utterance Examples

- rek mana — customer asks where or to which account/payment destination to send funds
- nomor rekening — customer asks where or to which account/payment destination to send funds
- bank apa — customer asks where or to which account/payment destination to send funds
- kirim ke mana — customer asks where or to which account/payment destination to send funds
- rekening yang mana — customer asks where or to which account/payment destination to send funds

## Required Context and Routing

Required context or entities: requested payment method; active approved destination from config/backend
Default bot action: Return account details only from approved backend/config; never invent or alter numbers.
Backend or tool requirement: Approved payment destination/config service
Escalation triggers: No valid destination, conflict with context, or transfer sent elsewhere.
Success signal: Correct approved destination delivered

## Behavior Rules

### Customer asks destination
Required action: Return approved active payment details.
Do not: Never invent, alter, or recall account numbers from memory.
Required data or tool: Approved payment destination/config service
Escalate if: No valid destination or conflicting context
Example response: “Siap bos, ini yang aktif: [PAYMENT_METHOD] — [PAYMENT_DESTINATION].”

### Requested destination unavailable
Required action: Check another approved active option.
Do not: Do not provide stale destination.
Required data or tool: Approved config service
Escalate if: No approved alternative
Example response: “Yang ini lagi nggak tersedia bos. Saya cek opsi yang aktif dulu ya.”

## Ambiguity Guidance

### Compared with Top Up / Deposit (INT_TOPUP)
Why they overlap: Top-up flow often includes asking where to transfer.
Prefer this intent when: User specifically asks for bank/account/payment destination.
Prefer Top Up / Deposit when: User wants to begin/add balance and amount/flow is the main need.
Context to check: Whether amount/top-up was already established in prior turn
Classifier guidance: Choose the narrowest immediate need; Payment Details can be a substep of Top Up.

### Compared with General Help / How-to (INT_GENERAL_HELP)
Why they overlap: “bisa pake bank apa” is informational while “rek mana” asks for a live destination.
Prefer this intent when: User asks for the current active destination/account to send money to.
Prefer General Help / How-to when: User asks supported methods or general process.
Context to check: Question asks capability vs specific live destination
Classifier guidance: General Help uses approved knowledge; Payment Details requires live config/backend.

## Response Style Examples

“Siap bos, ini yang aktif: [PAYMENT_METHOD] — [PAYMENT_DESTINATION]. Transfer sesuai nominal ya, kalau udah kirim buktinya.”
If unavailable: “Yang ini lagi nggak tersedia bos. Saya cek opsi yang aktif dulu ya.”
Never type account numbers from memory; placeholders must come from system/config.

Dialogue examples guide tone and behavior only. They are not proof of live status or factual truth. Live facts must come from approved configuration or backend tools.
