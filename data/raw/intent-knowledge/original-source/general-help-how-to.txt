General Help / How-to Intent Playbook

Intent ID: INT_GENERAL_HELP
Priority: P1
Source type: Observed frontliner-derived taxonomy

## Retrieval Profile

Customer goal: Understand process, rules, requirements, limits, or next step.
Classification cues: caranya gimana; minimal berapa; bisa pake bank apa; gimana prosesnya; harus apa
Semantic retrieval text: Intent: General Help / How-to. Customer goal: Understand process, rules, requirements, limits, or next step. Typical phrases: caranya gimana; minimal berapa; bisa pake bank apa; gimana prosesnya; harus apa. Meaning: customer asks how something works, requirements, limits, supported methods, or next steps.

## Required Context and Routing

Required context or entities: exact question; relevant approved knowledge/config; account context only when needed
Default bot action: Answer exact question briefly, use known context, and transition into relevant flow when requested.
Backend or tool requirement: Approved knowledge/config; account context when relevant
Escalation triggers: Policy ambiguous, account-specific data unavailable, or exception requested.
Success signal: Answered without unnecessary follow-up

## Behavior Rules

### Known factual/config question
Required action: Answer exactly and briefly from approved knowledge/config.
Do not: Do not invent limits, methods, or rules.
Required data or tool: Approved knowledge/config
Escalate if: Policy ambiguous or exception requested
Example response: “Bisa bos. Untuk top up, sebut nominalnya dulu.”

### Customer wants to proceed
Required action: Transition into the relevant transaction flow.
Do not: Do not ask unrelated follow-up questions.
Required data or tool: Relevant flow/context
Example response: “Siap, kirim nominalnya ya bos.”

## Ambiguity Guidance

### Compared with Payment / Account Details (INT_PAYMENT_DETAILS)
Why they overlap: “bisa pake bank apa” is informational while “rek mana” asks for a live destination.
Prefer this intent when: User asks supported methods or general process.
Prefer Payment / Account Details when: User asks for the current active destination/account to send money to.
Context to check: Question asks capability vs specific live destination
Classifier guidance: General Help uses approved knowledge; Payment Details requires live config/backend.

## Response Style Examples

Answer the exact question briefly, e.g. “Bisa bos. Untuk top up, sebut nominalnya dulu.” / “Minimal [SYSTEM_MINIMUM] bos.” / “Bisa lewat [AVAILABLE_METHODS].”
If customer wants to proceed: “Siap, kirim nominalnya ya bos.”
All limits/methods must come from approved config.

Dialogue examples guide tone and behavior only. They are not proof of live status or factual truth. Live facts must come from approved configuration or backend tools.
