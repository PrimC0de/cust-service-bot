Migration Notes

## Scope
The eight original intent playbooks are preserved unchanged under original-source/.

## Structural Changes
- intent-catalog.txt contains one canonical record per intent.
- atomic-utterance-examples.txt contains one independently retrievable customer utterance per row.
- behavior-rules.txt separates operational behavior and approved facts from utterance examples.
- ambiguity-map.txt records near-neighbor intent pairs and their disambiguating signals.
- knowledge-gaps.txt separates unknown, live, or unverified facts from authoritative knowledge.

## Migration Rules
- Exact duplicates within the same intent were removed.
- The duplicate phrase "rek mana" remains under INT_TOPUP and INT_PAYMENT_DETAILS intentionally because it is a documented ambiguity, not weighting duplication.
- Ambiguous or continuation-style examples are marked requires_context=true.
- Dialogue examples and observed frontliner behavior are not promoted into authoritative business facts.
- Approved Royal Dream and bongkar facts remain explicit behavior rules with their original notation preserved.
