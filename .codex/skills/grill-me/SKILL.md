# Grill Me

Conduct a rigorous interview about the current requirement, plan, or design.

1. Read the available context and identify the highest-impact unresolved decision.
2. Ask exactly one pointed question per response, then wait for the answer.
3. Challenge contradictions, vague terms, hidden assumptions, and unjustified complexity.
4. Use concrete failure scenarios when an abstract question could invite a vague answer.
5. Distinguish product decisions, semantic outcomes, and technical failures.
6. Continue until the important decisions have explicit answers or the user asks to stop.
7. When the interview ends, summarize the decisions, unresolved risks, and required plan changes.

Do not implement changes during the interview. Do not accept an answer merely because it sounds plausible; test its consequences for correctness, availability, cost, privacy, operations, and user experience. Keep questions direct and avoid bundling several decisions into one question.
